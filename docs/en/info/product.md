# Packages & Compatibility

This page helps you confirm the package contents and usage environment before purchasing. After receiving your devices, start with [Quick Start](/en/guide/quick-start) to connect the receiver, power on the trackers and get them recognized in SlimeVR.

## Package Contents

| Item | 6-point | 8-point | 10-point | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Trackers | 6 | 8 | 10 | Capture body movement |
| 2.4G receiver | 1 | 1 | 1 | Receive tracker data |
| GoPro chest mount | 1 | 1 | 1 | Hold the chest tracker |
| 30 cm strap | 2 | 4 | 6 | Shins, arms, feet and other points |
| 56 cm strap | 2 | 2 | 2 | Thigh trackers |
| 86 cm strap | 1 | 1 | 1 | Hip tracker |
| Charging dock | 1 | 1 | 1 | Charging and batch control |
| Matching clips | 6 | 8 | 10 | Secure the trackers to the straps |
| Stickers | 1 | 1 | 1 | Mark the assigned tracker positions |

## Choosing a Point Setup

| Setup | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| 6 points | First-time users who want stable basic full-body tracking. We recommend everyone start here to learn the flow. |
| 8 points | Users who can already complete the 6-point wearing and calibration reliably and want more movement detail. |
| 10 points | Users familiar with wearing positions, model skeleton adjustment and calibration, with a clear need for more points. |

More recommended positions can be found in [Assembly & Wearing](/en/guide/assembly).

## Usage Environment

- The main flows in this manual target a combination of Windows, SlimeVR, SteamVR and VRChat.
- The trackers communicate with the computer through the 2.4G receiver; attach the antenna to the receiver and connect it to a USB port before use.
- The trackers supplement the body points; the head and hands are usually tracked by the headset and VR controllers.
- Before use, confirm that SlimeVR, SteamVR, VRChat and your headset environment are all working normally.

::: warning Special requirements before purchasing
If you need to confirm a specific headset, wireless streaming method, operating system, or an additional-point plan, contact [support](/en/support/) with your device model and usage scenario before ordering.
:::

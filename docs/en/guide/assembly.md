# Assembly & Wearing

How securely you wear the trackers and whether their orientation matches the diagrams directly affects calibration and tracking quality. This page covers the GoPro chest mount, strap clips and recommended placement points.

## GoPro Chest Mount Assembly

1. Prepare the chest mount, fixing clip and a tracker.
2. Push the clip into the chest-mount socket following the arrow on the clip.
3. Slide the tracker in along the slot and confirm the clip is locked.

Do not force the clip in the wrong direction, or you may damage it.
:::

## Strap Assembly

1. Prepare the strap, tracker clip and quick-release clip.
2. With the silicone anti-slip side of the strap facing down, attach the strap to the tracker clip.
3. Snap in the quick-release clip in the correct direction.
4. Adjust the length, then close the hook-and-loop fastener.

## Recommended Wearing Points

In the original manual, red markers indicate the recommended 6-point positions, and green markers indicate the extra positions for 8 or 10 points.

| Setup | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 6 points | Base recommended setup; wear according to the red markers |
| 8 points | Adds two extra points on top of the 6-point setup |
| 10 points | Adds two more points on top of the 8-point setup |

## Wearing Checklist

- Trackers on left and right symmetric positions should be at roughly the same height.
- Straps should fit snugly without restricting blood flow or joint movement.
- Tracker orientation should match the diagrams.
- After intense movement, if any strap has shifted, re-run the wearing calibration.

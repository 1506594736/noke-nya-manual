## Adding Trackers

1. First confirm with support that your existing tracker model, receiver and firmware are compatible with the new devices.
2. Provide your original package order info when adding points, as requested by support.
3. Confirm whether straps and clips are included and how shipping is calculated.

After receiving the new devices, follow [Add or Replace Trackers](/en/guide/add-tracker) to pair, assign points and calibrate.
